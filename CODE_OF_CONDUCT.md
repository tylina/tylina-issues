# Community guidelines / 社区交流准则

Keep feedback specific, patient, and respectful. Discuss the product behavior and the writing problem; do not attack or speculate about other people.

请保持具体、耐心和尊重。讨论产品行为与写作问题，不攻击他人，也不揣测他人动机。

Remove private information from examples. Do not post harassment, spam, unlawful material, or content unrelated to Tylina. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that does not follow these guidelines.

请从示例中删除私人信息。不要发布骚扰、垃圾信息、违法内容或与 Tylina 无关的内容。维护者可以编辑、隐藏、锁定或删除不符合准则的内容。
